Meet Our Therapists

Our team consists of licensed professionals with expertise in various mental health specialties. Each therapist brings unique skills and approaches to help you on your healing journey.

Dr. Asha Verma

PhD, Licensed Clinical Psychologist

Specialties: Anxiety, Relationship Counseling, Women's Issues

With over 12 years of experience, Dr. Verma specializes in cognitive-behavioral approaches to anxiety and relationship challenges. She creates a warm, non-judgmental space for clients to explore their concerns.

Book with Dr. Verma

Dr. Rajiv Singh

PsyD, Licensed Psychologist

Specialties: Depression, PTSD, Trauma Recovery

Dr. Singh combines evidence-based trauma therapies with mindfulness techniques to help clients heal from painful experiences. His approach is both compassionate and results-oriented.

Book with Dr. Singh

Dr. Neha Kapoor

EdD, Licensed Counselor

Specialties: Teen Therapy, Stress Management, Life Transitions

Dr. Kapoor specializes in working with adolescents and young adults navigating academic stress, social challenges, and major life changes. Her approach is collaborative and strength-based.

Book with Dr. Kapoor

Dr. Amit Desai

MD, Psychiatrist

Specialties: Addiction, Medication Management, Dual Diagnosis

Dr. Desai provides both therapy and medication management for clients dealing with substance use and co-occurring mental health conditions. He emphasizes holistic recovery approaches.

Book with Dr. Desai

Ms. Priya Iyer

LMFT, Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist

Specialties: Couples Counseling, Family Therapy, Communication Skills

With a focus on relationships, Ms. Iyer helps couples and families improve communication, resolve conflicts, and strengthen their connections through practical, solution-focused therapy.

Book with Ms. Iyer
