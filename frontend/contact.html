Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prepare for my first session?

For your first session, find a quiet, private space with good internet connection. Have any questions or topics you want to discuss ready. Your therapist will guide you through the rest.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

You can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your session without charge through your account. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person?

Research shows online therapy can be just as effective as in-person for many mental health concerns. The key is finding the right therapist and being committed to the process.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, UPI payments, and net banking. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Are my sessions confidential?

Yes, all sessions are completely confidential. We use encrypted video platforms and adhere to strict privacy policies in compliance with healthcare regulations.
