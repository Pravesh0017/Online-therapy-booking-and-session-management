Our Story

Mindful Therapy was founded in 2023 with a simple mission: to make quality mental health care accessible to everyone, regardless of location or schedule constraints. Our founder, Dr. Sanjay Patel, recognized the growing need for flexible, stigma-free therapy options after years of clinical practice.

What began as a small practice with three therapists has grown into a network of over 50 licensed professionals across India, serving thousands of clients each month. We're proud to have helped people from all walks of life find healing and growth through our platform.

Our Approach

At Mindful Therapy, we believe in a client-centered approach that honors each individual's unique experiences and needs. Our therapy methods are evidence-based and tailored to help you achieve your personal goals.

We integrate various therapeutic modalities including:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)
  • Psychodynamic Therapy
  • Solution-Focused Brief Therapy
  • Person-Centered Therapy

Our therapists work collaboratively with you to determine the most effective approach for your situation.
